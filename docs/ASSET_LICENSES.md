Generated placeholders (no third-party source): placeholder UI tones are synthesized at runtime in `core/autoload/audio.gd`; sandbox geometry uses Godot primitive meshes. Step 3: weapon, impact, grenade and hit sounds are synthesized at runtime (`core/audio/weapon_audio.gd`); greybox weapon models are built from primitives (`core/weapons/greybox_gun.gd`); the decal texture is generated in code (`core/vfx/weapon_fx.gd`). No third-party assets were added.

Step 4 (all generated in-house; **no third-party assets**): the Vesper modular kit (`game/content/kits/vesper/*.glb`), look-dev props (`game/content/props/*/*.glb`), the orderly zombie model and the zombie animation library (`game/content/enemies/zombie/`, `game/content/characters/animations/`), the M1911 viewmodel and world model (`game/content/weapons/m1911/*.glb`) are generated by the project's own scripts in `tools/blender/` (Blender output belongs to the author of the input — here the project). Shaders (`game/content/shaders/*.gdshader`), materials, environment and quality presets are hand-written project files; the detail noise is a Godot `NoiseTexture2D` generated at load. Ambience beds (storm wind, room tone) are synthesized at runtime (`core/audio/ambience_library.gd`). The look-dev scene is generated by `tools/lookdev/build_lookdev.gd`. The Godot skeleton profile/BoneMap are engine built-ins. Unknown-license files: none.

Step 5 (**no third-party assets**): zombie voices, footsteps, gib, body-fall, barricade and door sounds are synthesized at runtime (`core/audio/zombie_audio.gd`); barricade planks, nav doors, gib chunks and the nav torture map are built from Godot primitives in code; ragdolls are Godot physical bones generated per death. Unknown-license files: none.

Step 7 (**no third-party assets**): the four survivor models (`game/content/characters/<id>/<id>.glb`) and the survivor animation library (`game/content/characters/animations/survivor_anims.glb`) are generated by `tools/blender/characters/build_survivors.py`; character materials are hand-written project files; lobby / ping cues are synthesized at runtime (`core/audio/coop_audio.gd`). GodotSteam (MIT) is **not** vendored — if a human installs it (docs/NETWORKING.md §3), add its row here. Unknown-license files: none.

Step 9 (**no third-party assets**): the 17 new weapon definitions are generated by `tools/gen_weapons.py`; greybox+ weapon models, projectiles, tripmines, the Relic Box trunk, light pillar and porcelain doll and the stencil-on-canvas wall-buys are built from Godot primitives in code; the music-box melody (an original figure written for the project), bell chimes, doll laugh, lid slam, decoy drum, mine beeps and interior gun tails are synthesized at runtime (`core/box/relic_box_audio.gd`, `core/audio/weapon_audio.gd`). Unknown-license files: none.

Step 13 (**no third-party assets**): the 11 decal textures in `game/content/decals/` (grime, water stain, blood, frost, scorch, claws, handprints, tally, the Choir notation, scratched cross, hazard stripes) are drawn procedurally by `tools/art/gen_decals.py` (numpy + PIL, fixed seeds); the night sky, cloud layers and wind sway are original shaders (`content/shaders/sky_night|cloud_layer|wind_sway.gdshader`); every prop of `maps/vesper_peak/scripts/vesper_props.gd`, the mountain ring, the radar tower lattice, the glass dome and the Sky Bridge rails are built from Godot primitives in code; the sanatorium / Group Vesper signage text is original; the ambience beds (furnace, drips, machinery, refrigeration, reactor heartbeat, radio static, fluorescent buzz, gust swell) are synthesized at runtime (`core/audio/ambience_library.gd`). Unknown-license files: none.
