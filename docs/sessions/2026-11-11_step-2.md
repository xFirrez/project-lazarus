## Completed
- **PlayerCharacter** (`core/player/player_character.tscn`): `CharacterBody3D` with `MotorComponent`, `CameraRig`, `HealthComponent`, `FootstepComponent`; local player = first-person camera + `DamageHud`; remote players = interpolated puppets (capsule + head + visor, crouch scaling, spatial footsteps). `TempCapsule` removed.
- **PlayerTuning** resource (`content/player/player_tuning.tres`): every movement/camera/health/validation number; `tune <param> <value>` edits it live.
- **MotorComponent:** acceleration 45 m/s² / friction 9, air control 8 m/s², sprint 4.0 s stamina with 0.8 s + 3.0 s recharge (ends on fire/ADS, release-to-restart after exhaustion), crouch 1.8 → 1.1 m capsule with ceiling check, slide (0.7 s, 7.5 → 3.0 m/s, 1.0 s cooldown, steerable, ends in crouch under low geometry), jump (1.0 m apex, coyote time + buffer), mantle ≤ 1.2 m measured from the last floor (path swept clear before the scripted move), step-up ≤ 0.35 m with camera step smoothing, slopes ≤ 46°, moving-platform carry, teleport auto-crouch under low geometry, noclip.
- **CameraRig:** yaw/pitch (±89°), horizontal FOV setting (70–110) + 5° sprint/slide kick, head bob and strafe tilt (bob scale), landing dip spring, crouch/slide eye-height transitions (0.15 s), trauma shake (shake scale). Look applied per render frame (D-018).
- **Health & feedback:** HP 100, regen after 3.0 s at 100 HP/s, damage API (amount, source position, type), fall damage (5–10 m → 0–50), downed hook (placeholder restore after 3 s until Step 6). `DamageHud`: vignette shader (`content/shaders/damage_vignette.gdshader`) scaling with missing HP + hit flash, directional wedges, placeholder crosshair; heartbeat + low-pass muffling of SFX/Weapons/Zombies buses below 50% (`Audio.set_muffle`).
- **Footsteps:** `SurfaceTags` (collider metadata or PhysicsMaterial `resource_name`), `FootstepLibrary` synthesized placeholder walk/sprint/land sets for concrete, metal, wood, snow, gravel, tile; remote steps via the new `Audio.play_event_3d`.
- **Networking:** state at 30 Hz now carries pitch, stance/grounded/sprint flags and a correction sequence; host `MovementValidator` (distance budget, D-016) corrects speed hacks and teleports; host-authoritative health events, fall-damage claims and host-authorized teleports (D-017).
- **Movement course** (`maps/_test/movement_course.tscn`, map id `movement_course`): 20 m sprint lane with timing gates, 0.15/0.30/0.35 m stairs, 20°/30°/45° ramps (+ unwalkable 50°), 1.0/1.2 m mantle ledges and a 1.5 m non-mantleable ledge, 0.8 m gap, doorway, 1.3 m slide tunnel, 1.5 m low ceiling, call elevator to a 10 m deck with 5 m shelf (5 m and 10 m drops) with a safety stop, horizontal shuttle platform, perimeter walls, 21 bot waypoints, 3 screenshot shots.
- **Input:** `PlayerInputFrame` / `PlayerInputSource` (raw mouse, ADS multiplier, invert Y, hold/toggle sprint and crouch from Config, gamepad radial deadzone + power curve). `BotInputDriver`: waypoint routes, direction driver, deterministic random driver.
- **Console:** `tune`, `player_info`, `damage`, `heal`, `god`, `noclip`, `tp`, `respawn`, `speed_hack`, `setting`, `settings_save`.
- **Tools:** integration suite runs with `--fixed-fps 60` (D-019); `tools/net_tests.sh` gained `speedhack` and `course` (generic `pair_test`); new `tools/visual_tests.sh` (rendered HUD check).

## Decisions & deviations
- D-016 … D-020 added to ARCHITECTURE §14 (validation budget + correction sequence, health events, render-rate look, fixed-fps integration tests, local test-map platforms).
- New tuning values not given in GAME_DESIGN §3.1 (gravity, coyote time, eye heights, bob, validation limits, …) and the sprint restart rule are recorded in the GAME_DESIGN tuning log.
- Settings (#8) live in `Config` and the `setting` console command; menu UI deferred to Step 15 (Deferrals table).
- Low-health muffling applies to SFX, Weapons and Zombies buses (the plan says "SFX bus"); otherwise gunfire would stay un-muffled from Step 3 on.
- Mantle triggers on jump + forward against a ledge, or automatically when walking forward into a ledge ≤ 1.2 m (avoids sticking on crates, GAME_DESIGN §3.1).
- AC-2.3 counts static geometry only; a rider can read up to one frame of platform motion as overlap because the check runs before the character resolves that frame (documented in the test).
- Found and fixed during testing: stair step-up used a smaller safe margin than `move_and_slide` (stuck on 0.35 m stairs from some approach angles); the mantle could clip block sides on diagonal approaches (now swept); the course elevator could descend onto a player (safety sensor).
