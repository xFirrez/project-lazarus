## Completed
- **Balance (16A):** `game/tests/balance/balance_model.gd` is a deterministic model over the shipped data. It covers the weapon TTK tiers, the economy purchase plan per VESPER_PEAK §2.4 (solo and co-op, shared doors) and the round curve to r200. It feeds `tools/run_sim.sh balance` (`docs/BALANCE_REPORT.md`) and `tests/unit/test_balance.gd`. Tuning: Model 37 130 → 160 × 8 and .357 Revolver 160 → 210 (both had been weaker than the 500-point wall-buys). Results: wall 8.5 → box 17 → upgraded 24–29 → wonder 41–50 (median "viable until" round). All §6.3 milestones fall within ±2 rounds, 1–2 rounds early. §6.3 "4,000 by r5" is now read as spending power (proposed design change).
- **High rounds:** `run_sim.sh high_rounds` (with `--plan=`) runs 4 god-mode bots with upgraded weapons. It plays rounds 1–3, jumps to 10…50, then plays 55–60, and spawns the Warden at the last round.
